## About me

<img
  align="right"
  alt="AI Engineering"
  width="360"
  src="https://cdn.dribbble.com/users/1162077/screenshots/3848914/programmer.gif"
/>

I build agentic AI systems and production RAG pipelines that ship into real products. My work focuses on LLM orchestration, multi-agent workflows, and backend engineering for reliable AI applications.

I work with **LangGraph**, **LangChain**, **AutoGen**, **CrewAI**, **Qdrant**, **pgVector**, and **FastAPI**. I am especially interested in document intelligence, agent communication, observability, and production-ready AI systems.

Open to collaborating on agentic AI projects and applied multi-agent research.
